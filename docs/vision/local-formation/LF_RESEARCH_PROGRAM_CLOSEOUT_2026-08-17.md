## Scope

This record closes the LF-1, LF-2, and LF-3 research program without further
manual review, annotation work, profile search, morphology research, or model
experimentation.

The existing LF research outputs have one bounded role:

`Deterministic local formation region/candidate proposal`

This role applies to the research outputs only. It does not add or modify a
public Vision contract, and it does not change current Vision production
behavior.

## Founder Decision

- LF-1, LF-2, and LF-3 are closed.
- No additional manual alignment review or annotation work is required.
- The existing candidate proposal behavior is not declared unsuccessful.
- The existing evidence is not presented as pixel-perfect physical
  segmentation validation.
- No LF profile is promoted into Vision production by this closeout.
- No new threshold sweep, morphology study, or local-formation profile search
  is approved.
- A learned image model is not justified by the current evidence and remains
  deferred.
- Existing local candidate proposals may be used in later Atlas research and
  integration work within the bounded role above.
- LF may be reopened only when material phone or product usage supplies new,
  concrete failure evidence.

## Interpretation of Existing Results

The LF-1, LF-2, and LF-3 observations remain valid records of the exact tested
methods. Their results show that the tested profiles did not satisfy the
research program's strict human-alignment and pixel-perfect localization
target.

The final `0 aligned` outcome is not treated as conclusive evidence that the
candidate proposal behavior is algorithmically useless. Review terminology
may have been interpreted more strictly or differently than intended, while
the Founder observed that the system marked useful residue regions in many
images.

Accordingly, prior statements that an exact evidence family was
"insufficient" remain method-scoped conclusions. They do not establish that
the current system as a whole failed, and they do not justify an automatic
transition to AI or semantic segmentation.

## Preserved Limitations

- Human review calibration remains uncertain.
- Ground truth uses bounding boxes rather than pixel-accurate masks.
- Negative-space evidence is represented by only six primary annotations.
- Pixel-perfect segmentation has not been validated.
- Only two eligible formation groups were available for repeated-formation
  consistency.
- Candidate proposal usefulness has not been converted into a production
  accuracy or generalization claim.

These limitations remain explicit but are not blockers for bounded downstream
use of the current candidate proposals.

## Production Boundary

This closeout changes no Vision, Pattern, Knowledge, Symbol, dataset, Android,
or public package behavior. It introduces no semantic identity, confidence,
ranking, interpretation, language generation, AI, or learned-model contract.

No claim is made that a production local-formation API exists. Research
candidate proposals remain non-canonical until a future, separately approved
architecture establishes otherwise.

## Reopen Gate

The LF program may be reopened only with new product evidence, such as a
repeatable phone workflow failure that materially prevents useful region
proposal. A reopen decision must define the observed failure before selecting
an algorithm, threshold, deterministic feature family, or learned model.
